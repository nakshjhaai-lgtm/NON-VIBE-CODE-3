Privacy Policy

What this site collects and what the practice does with it. Also how to ask for your own copy.

Last updated: March 1, 2026

Vance Dental Studio ("we", "us") runs the website at vance-dental.netlify.app and the clinical practice at 1200 Avenue of the Americas, Suite 400, New York, NY 10036. This policy covers personal information collected both online and in the office.

Information we collect

From the booking and contact forms we collect your name, email address, phone number and whatever you write in the message box. From treatment we hold appointment records and the insurance details needed to bill a claim. If you accept analytics cookies we also see technical data such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you opened and the time you spent on them.

How we use information

  • To schedule and provide dental care
  • To respond to inquiries within our stated response window
  • To process insurance claims and payments
  • To improve site performance and security
  • To send newsletters only when you opt in

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We pass data to dental laboratories, imaging centers, insurers and payment processors where your care or your bill needs it, and where the law requires it. Protected health information is handled under applicable HIPAA requirements.

Cookies

Essential cookies keep the site working; the theme preference you set is stored in one of them. Analytics cookies load only after you accept the banner. Rejecting them changes nothing about the pages you can read.

Retention

Clinical records are kept for as long as New York State requires. Newsletter addresses are deleted when you unsubscribe. Server logs are rotated on a short schedule.

Your choices

Email hello@vancedental.com to ask for a copy of what we hold. You can also have something corrected, or have it deleted. Clinical records we are legally required to keep are the one exception.
