About

Dr. Alistair Vance, DDS

A one-address practice in Midtown Manhattan. Appointments are kept long enough to finish the work properly, and materials are picked for how they hold up five years out.

Dr. Vance trained in restorative dentistry and implant placement, with a focus on occlusion. He has been practicing for 18 years. The diary is deliberately thin: an hour for a first appointment, and a written plan before anything irreversible happens.

There is one address and no second location. The hygiene team sees the same patients year after year, so the person charting your pockets has read last year's notes.

  • Preparation kept inside enamel wherever the case allows, and existing restorations repaired where they can be
  • Digital scanning and printed guides for implant cases where they improve accuracy
  • Written estimates, out-of-network PPO claims filed by the office
  • Third-party financing available for larger restorative plans

This site carries no team photographs. That is the studio's preference, so you will meet everyone at your first visit.
